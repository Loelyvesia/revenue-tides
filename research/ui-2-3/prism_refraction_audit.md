# Prism Refraction Audit

Direct state:

- the hard glass rectangle and calibration rails are removed;
- a horizontal SVG gradient mask creates a borderless reading field;
- the selected quarter uses one luminous bead and a large numeric readout;
- axis, all-quarter tick marks and default nodes are absent.

Overlap state:

- the legacy surface remains the incoming current;
- the royalty-integrated surface appears through the refraction mask only;
- the two values remain separate observations with separate metric versions;
- “same quarter / two reporting definitions / not a revenue split” is explicit;
- the previous dotted connector and double seam lines are removed.

Derived state retains point estimate, uncertainty interval and direction-stability language. Events remain on an independent context layer and are explicitly non-causal.
