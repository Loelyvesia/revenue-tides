# Before / after audit

## Hero

**Before:** two similarly weighted text columns made the opening read like a competent project summary. Scope facts were a single inline row.

**After:** the Chinese title is the dominant cover element; the thesis becomes the counterweight. Scope is now a typographic 09 / 75 / 04 / fiscal-window ledger, with a quiet calibration motif and a content cue rather than a button.

## Layout

**Before:** sections used broadly similar title-plus-container rhythms.

**After:** numbered editorial openings, full-width analytical stages, narrow evidence notes, ledger rows, and a research appendix create deliberate variation without changing navigation or information order.

## Typography

**Before:** headings were clear but compressed into a dashboard-like scale.

**After:** hero, section, Finding, value, annotation, and quarter scales are visibly distinct. Tabular figures and quarter labels remain numeric; Chinese body copy uses longer line height and controlled measure.

## Quarter Prism

**Before:** the Prism sat in a bordered panel and resembled a conventional chart followed by detail cards.

**After:** it is a calibrated reading stage. The selected quarter receives a wash, corner caps, a top label, and a stronger mark. The double definition seam is labeled, trajectories remain disconnected, readings use a two-column instrument layout, and source access is reduced to `SOURCE ↗`.

## Findings

**Before:** the three headline Findings shared one chapter structure and similarly sized visual containers.

**After:** F1 is a same-quarter split ledger, F2 is a large trajectory-led +837.3% composition with a separate event rail, and F4 is a long sequence emphasizing peak → drawdown → recovery/new peak → later drawdown.

## Observatory

**Before:** rows were functional but behaved visually like a dense table followed by a bordered detail card.

**After:** the Research Ledger uses a top rule, fine dividers, a calibration marker, quiet row wash, and a two-pixel arrow shift. Expanded details are an inline analytical canvas with border rules rather than a card.

## Mobile

**Before:** the mobile view was largely a single-column reduction of desktop, including a 1-column scope list and larger card-like Prism blocks.

**After:** the hero uses a 2×2 scope ledger, Prism controls become a compact three-part stepper, the selected reading is the conceptual screen, definition lanes stack clearly, and series rows reduce to two or three meaningful lines.
