# Derived-value policy

Derived official series are eligible but must remain visibly and analytically distinct from directly reported series.

Required fields include the official denominator, official numerator/share, source for every input, deterministic formula, rounding increment/assumption, point estimate, lower and upper uncertainty bounds, units, and verification status.

Derived values may support stable within-series change analysis. They must never be labeled `OFFICIAL_REPORTED` or described as exact disclosed title revenue.

Corpus composition requires at least two-thirds primarily `OFFICIAL_REPORTED` series and permits no more than one-third primarily `DERIVED_FROM_OFFICIAL` series. For a target of 10, approximately 8 direct and 2 derived is preferred; 9+1 is acceptable when evidence quality supports it.
