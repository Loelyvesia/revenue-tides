# Public-release contract

The project maintains two layers.

## Internal provenance

May contain local relative paths, hashes, raw official files, extraction notes, and QA status.

## Public data

May contain structured factual observations, original values and units, evidence class, formulas and uncertainty, page references, canonical source URLs, event dates, and concise scope notes.

Public data must not contain local machine paths, copied slides or charts, raw PDFs, mirrored IR libraries, authentication material, or internal QA notes not intended for release.

This separation is mandatory in every later phase. The policy is technical and provenance-focused, not a legal opinion.
