# Evidence policy

## Lane A — Direct official

`OFFICIAL_REPORTED` is an exact value explicitly disclosed for the analytical entity and period by the reporting company or an official regulatory filing. This is the preferred backbone.

## Lane B — Derived official

`DERIVED_FROM_OFFICIAL` is calculated solely from compatible official inputs using a transparent deterministic formula. Each record must preserve every input source, denominator/share, formula, rounding assumption, uncertainty interval, and verification status.

## Context

`EVENT_OFFICIAL` documents official product events. `ATTRIBUTED_CLAIM` preserves issuer explanations. `SECONDARY_CONTEXT` and `INDUSTRY_CONTEXT` may support background when citation and reuse are appropriate.

`INDUSTRY_ESTIMATE` is not permitted in the primary quantitative corpus and must never fill missing official quarters or be merged numerically with publisher-revenue series.
