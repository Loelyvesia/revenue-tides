# Findings admissibility

Phase F may produce evidence-bounded Findings from stable within-series change, persistence, volatility, drawdown/recovery, normalized relative trajectory, metric-version effects, official event timing, issuer-attributed explanations, and evidence/measurement structure.

Every Finding must specify supporting observations, entity, time window, metric version, evidence class, limitations, and whether derived values are involved.

Finding confidence classes are:

- `DIRECT`: supported directly by stable official observations.
- `DERIVED`: depends on a deterministic official derivation.
- `ATTRIBUTED`: reports a named issuer/official explanation.
- `DESCRIPTIVE`: a reproducible mathematical description.

No arbitrary confidence percentage or premature qualitative label such as “stable,” “explosive,” or “declining” is allowed before evidence-driven thresholds are validated.
