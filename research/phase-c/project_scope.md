# Project scope

## In scope

The project studies fiscal-quarter changes in officially disclosed commercial revenue metrics for publisher-defined `GAME_TITLE`, `GAME_EDITION`, `IP`, and `FRANCHISE` entities. It emphasizes within-series trajectory, persistence, volatility, drawdown, recovery, platform expansion, reporting-definition changes, and contemporaneous official events.

Official values are publisher accounting observations. They are not assumed to equal gross consumer spending or total worldwide game revenue.

## Out of scope

- Global total game revenue or consumer-spending reconstruction.
- Player-expenditure or market-share databases.
- “True revenue” rankings of games or publishers.
- Estimation of undisclosed title revenue.
- Steam owners multiplied by price.
- Sensor Tower or AppMagic numerical reconstruction.
- Paid/private databases, private APIs, unofficial leaks, or speculative platform splits.
- Causal impact estimation without a separately approved causal design.

Half-year and annual values are `CONTEXT_ONLY`. Quarterly totals must not be interpolated or reconstructed into months.
