# Forbidden claims

The project must not claim or imply:

- that a title is the world's highest-earning game;
- that one game earned more than another across incompatible publisher metrics;
- that the dataset represents global game revenue or true player spending;
- that a game has permanently declined when the observed window merely ends below a peak;
- that a platform launch or event caused a measured change without an approved causal design;
- that one publisher monetizes better than another from incompatible accounting series;
- that missing quarters represent zero revenue;
- that an industry estimate confirms official publisher revenue;
- that company or segment revenue belongs to a title without an official compatible allocation;
- that a derived point estimate was directly reported;
- that metric versions can be stitched or averaged;
- that undisclosed values can be inferred from bar heights, price/owner proxies, or neighboring periods.

Preferred language includes “officially reported revenue,” “publisher-reported IP revenue,” “derived from official disclosures,” “within the observed period,” “under the publisher's reporting definition,” “the series increased/decreased,” “the event occurred in the same quarter,” and “the publisher attributed…”.
