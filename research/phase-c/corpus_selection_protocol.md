# Corpus selection protocol

Phase D may investigate enough candidates to select the final corpus but must retain only 8–12 analytical series, with target 10 and hard maximum 12. Rejected-candidate audits may remain as research metadata.

Candidates are evaluated on source authority, continuity, metric clarity, entity clarity, exact-value availability, version stability, event-context availability, platform diversity, publisher diversity, deterministic reconstruction, and public-release safety.

Evidence quality outranks popularity and diversity quotas. A weak series must not be added solely to fill a publisher, genre, or platform category.

The five Phase B series remain eligible reference candidates but are not automatically mandatory. Nexon remains context-only unless exact page-labeled values satisfy all frozen rules. Phase D must enforce the direct/derived ratio and Launch Cohort cap after selection.
