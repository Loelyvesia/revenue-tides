# Corpus inclusion and exclusion

## Mandatory eligibility

A series is eligible only when all conditions hold:

1. Quantitative evidence is an official public publisher, company, regulatory, or filing source.
2. Entity identity is resolvable.
3. Metric economic meaning is documented.
4. Fiscal boundaries are resolvable.
5. Original value, unit, and currency are known.
6. Platform/entity scope is known or explicitly unresolved.
7. Reconstruction is deterministic.
8. Values are not inferred from chart geometry.
9. Metric versions are distinguishable.
10. A public-safe structured factual output is possible.

## Continuity

Core series require at least 6 consecutive fiscal quarters; 8 are preferred. A `LAUNCH_COHORT` series requires at least 4 consecutive quarters, an observed launch, exact official quantitative disclosure, clear analytical value, and an explicit short-history label. No more than 2 Launch Cohort series are allowed, and they may not form the majority.

## Corpus bounds

Target 10, minimum 8, maximum 12 analytical series. Evidence quality outranks brand popularity or diversity quotas.

## Explicit exclusions

Industry estimates, unresolved chart-derived amounts, private/paid data, leaks, speculative allocations, and any series that cannot satisfy public-safe deterministic reconstruction are excluded. Nexon remains `CONTEXT_ONLY` unless exact page-labeled values later satisfy every frozen rule.
