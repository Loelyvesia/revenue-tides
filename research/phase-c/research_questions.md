# Research questions

The frozen research questions are:

1. **Commercial trajectory:** How does an officially disclosed game/IP revenue series change across consecutive fiscal quarters?
2. **Persistence:** Do elevated or depressed levels persist across multiple quarters or rapidly revert?
3. **Volatility:** How uneven is a series relative to its own history?
4. **Recovery:** After a local decline, does a later observation recover toward or beyond a prior internal level?
5. **Product/platform context:** How do launches, platform expansions, major updates, anniversaries, and other official events align temporally with quarterly change?
6. **Reporting semantics:** How can reporting-definition changes alter the apparent trajectory?
7. **Evidence structure:** How do exact official observations differ methodologically from transparent derivations from official inputs?

These questions do not predetermine Findings, thresholds, or narrative categories.
