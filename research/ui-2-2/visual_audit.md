## Screen review

| Screen | First focus | Three-second reading | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | title and tidal field | same title can have different reported numbers | PASS |
| Measurement | active question and diagram | four conditions for reading a number | PASS |
| Prism | selected quarter and value | value, definition, event and source | PASS |

| Notes | three direct conclusions | rebound, decline window, rounding uncertainty | PASS |

Hero foreground ribbons were reduced to four while all nine remain in the field. Measurement spacing is shorter and its active instrument is more solid. F1 gives Lineage M the dominant comparison and moves 2M/W into supporting evidence. F2 reduces the percentage’s dominance and removes the causal-looking pulse. F4 keeps its successful ribbon while adding four readable key values. Archive details now share one editorial canvas rather than a floating card.

No new effect family was added. The pass edits hierarchy, opacity, spacing, typography, and composition.
