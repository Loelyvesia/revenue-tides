## Outcome

Phase H defines a complete pre-implementation experience specification. The narrative begins with measurement semantics, teaches the model through Quarter Prism, presents F1/F2/F4 as editorial headline chapters, opens the nine-series Observatory, completes F3/F5/F6, and ends with method/evidence/source access.

Quarter Prism decomposes a selected fiscal quarter into value, entity scope, metric version, evidence class, uncertainty, events, issuer attribution, and source trail. Lineage overlap creates parallel lanes; derived evidence creates interval geometry; events remain on a separate context rail. Dragging is optional, mobile becomes a stacked quarter stepper, and reduced-motion mode presents final states immediately.

## Semantic preservation

- `GAME_TITLE` and `IP` remain visible entity types.
- Direct and derived observations use different mark structures, not a quality hierarchy.
- Legacy and royalty-integrated versions never form one continuous trajectory.
- Derived values retain compatible bounds, share-rounding context, and sign stability.
- Events are curated, non-exhaustive, and non-causal.
- F1–F6 retain their frozen claims, limits, windows, and source routes.
- Cross-company absolute scales, USD normalization, rankings, trend labels, and success scores are absent.

## Visual system
