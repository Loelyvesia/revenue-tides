# Design risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation frozen for Phase H | Residual status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quarter Prism becomes too complex | Medium | High | Default guided sequence exposes one concept at a time; persistent decomposition order; direct state first; full source detail stays in drawer | Controlled |
| Financial metaphor resembles stock trading | Medium | High | Warm paper, ledger rules, fiscal labels, calibration seams, neutral movement marks; no candlesticks, live ticker, red/green trading semantics, or terminal chrome | Controlled |
| Derived uncertainty feels too technical | High | High | Show a point, compatible interval, zero reference, and plain label “Direction not robust to rounding”; retain the share note on demand | Controlled |
| Version break is misunderstood as an error | Medium | High | Double calibration seam uses neutral graphite; label “Reporting definition changed”; no red/error icon; baseline restarts visibly | Controlled |
| Event marker is read as causal | High | High | Separate context rail, “same quarter” wording, permanent non-causal note, no connector or impact score | Controlled |
| Mobile Prism becomes too dense | High | High | Replace desktop geometry with a quarter stepper and stacked decomposition; one series/quarter at a time; 44 px controls | Controlled |
| Methodology overwhelms narrative | Medium | Medium | S1 teaches five concepts briefly; details move to S6 and contextual evidence drawer | Controlled |
| Nine-series exploration becomes dashboard-like | Medium | High | Structured ledger/index with progressive disclosure; no nine-card wall, ranking, or global absolute chart | Controlled |
| F4 is flattened into “decline” | Medium | High | Required five-stage trajectory and labels; final status explicitly “not recovered within observed window” | Controlled |
| F5 accidentally extends across legacy | Medium | High | Finding mode activates only royalty-integrated v2 after the boundary; no cross-version line segment | Controlled |
| Color-only encoding fails accessibility | Low | High | Every category adds shape, stroke, pattern, label, and placement; contrast targets are explicit | Controlled |
| Long source/title text clips | Medium | Medium | Wrap titles; source drawer uses labeled links rather than raw URLs; mobile definition-list layout | Controlled |
| Lazy-load failure looks like missing research data | Low | Medium | Distinct loading and error copy; retain index identity; never substitute zeros or fallback values | Controlled |
