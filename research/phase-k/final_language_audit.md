# Final Language Audit

## Result

PASS. Judge-facing materials were reviewed for unsupported claims, false precision, causal language, permanent-decline language, and inconsistent terminology.

## Reviewed sensitive terms

The audit searched for Chinese terms including `真实流水`, `全球流水`, `全球游戏收入`, `最赚钱`, `最成功`, `冠军`, `霸榜`, `证明导致`, `导致收入增长`, `PC带来`, and `永久衰退`, and English terms including `winner`, `highest earning`, `true revenue`, `global revenue`, `caused`, and `permanent decline`.

Occurrences retained in the final materials are negations, questions, limitations, or forbidden-interpretation explanations. No material says that the project reconstructs true global revenue, ranks cross-company absolute revenue, proves that an event caused a change, or establishes permanent decline.

## Terminology

- Publisher-defined revenue/accounting/commercial disclosure remains distinct from consumer spending.
- `OFFICIAL_REPORTED` remains distinct from `DERIVED_FROM_OFFICIAL`.
- `GAME_TITLE` remains distinct from `IP`.
- Legacy and royalty-integrated remain explicit metric versions.
- “Not recovered within observed window” is used instead of permanent-decline language.
- Issuer explanations remain attributed claims.

## Editorial review

The Chinese summaries and pitches were read aloud for cadence and simplified where a formal research phrase could be stated directly. Repeated slogans, claims of novelty, promotional superlatives, and unnecessary English jargon were removed. Numeric precision matches the frozen Findings.
