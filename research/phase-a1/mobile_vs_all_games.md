# Mobile-only versus all-games official disclosure

## Option M — Mobile-only

Verified exact backbone:

- Goddess of Victory: NIKKE — 8 quarters.
- Lineage M — 6 quarters on the legacy scope.
- Lineage 2M — 6 quarters on the legacy scope.
- Lineage W — 6 quarters on the legacy scope.

Verified derived addition:

- MARVEL Contest of Champions — 6 quarters from official total revenue × rounded official title share.

Strengths: maintains the original mobile theme and provides several long-running live-service titles.

Weaknesses: only four exact series, heavy concentration in NCSOFT and SHIFT UP, fewer reporting companies, a material NCSOFT definition break in 2026, and greater dependence on rounded-share derivations to expand the corpus. Official disclosures also describe publisher revenue, not worldwide mobile consumer spending.

Assessment: **feasible only as a narrow Korean-publisher case study; not the strongest public-release project**.

## Option G — All games with official product-level disclosure

Adds:

- Stellar Blade — 8 exact quarters across console and PC.
- Black Desert IP — 8 exact quarters across PC, console, and mobile.
- New-game exceptions such as AION 2 and Lineage Classic as their histories mature.

Strengths: more companies, more exact values, longer clean series, meaningful platform-launch events, and less reliance on a single mobile reporting convention.

Weaknesses: the analytical entity is sometimes an IP rather than one app; cross-company level comparisons require stricter scope notes.

Assessment: **strongest evidence-led scope**.

## Recommendation

Adopt Option G and the title **《付费潮汐——游戏商业生命线》**. Keep platform scope explicit so the future interface can filter mobile, PC, console, or mixed-IP series without pretending they are identical.
