# Source rights and public-release notes

These notes document observed access and reuse signals. They are not legal advice.

## General release policy

- Prefer original structured factual observations with source title, canonical URL, publication date, retrieval date, and exact PDF page.
- Do not reproduce or distribute full IR decks, screenshots, charts, or site data feeds unless the source gives a clear license or separate permission is obtained.
- Keep official publisher-recognized revenue distinct from store spending, billings, bookings, royalties, and third-party estimates.
- Public output should contain concise factual labels and arithmetic, not copied expressive text.

## NCSOFT

- Public archive: https://m-corporate.nc.com/kr/ir/irArchive/earningsRelease.do
- Access: no login; official PDFs and XLS/XLSX factsheet links are public.
- Observed license signal: copyrighted corporate materials; no general open-data license found during this audit.
- Retention recommendation: source URL and page-level observation are sufficient for Phase A.1. In Phase B, retain only deduplicated quarter documents needed for reconstruction and hash them.
- Redistribution recommendation: derived structured facts and citations only; no deck mirroring by default.

## SHIFT UP

- Public archive: https://shiftup.co.kr/eng/ir/ir.php?code=ir_eng&idx=475&page=1&ptype=list
- Access: no login; presentation and factsheet attachments are directly public.
- Observed license signal: website footer states all rights reserved; factsheets include investor-use disclaimers but no open redistribution license.
- Retention recommendation: page-3 factsheet evidence is deterministic. If retained later, one factsheet can cover several quarters and should replace duplicates.
- Redistribution recommendation: publish values, units, page citations, and formulas—not copied pages.

## Netmarble

- Public archive: https://company.netmarble.com/en/invest/library
- Official PDF host: https://sgimage.netmarble.com/
- Access: no login. The desktop archive uses a public page data request; search-indexed official PDFs and official newsroom releases remain directly accessible.
- Observed license signal: official copyrighted presentations; no open-data license identified.
- Retention recommendation: store only the deck used for each quarter if later authorized. Title-share derivations must cite both the total-revenue page and game-portfolio page.
- Redistribution recommendation: publish formulas and rounding intervals; do not imply that the calculated point estimate was directly reported.

## Nexon

- Public archive: https://www.nexon.co.jp/en/ir/quarterly-disclosure/
- Access: no login; archive content is populated by IRPocket.
- Observed restriction: the public IRPocket data response states that unauthorized reproduction, transfer, sale, or other secondary use is prohibited.
- Phase A.1 action: the feed was inspected only to confirm the restriction and was not saved or transformed into a project dataset.
- Redistribution recommendation: link to the public archive and individual official documents. Do not mirror the feed. Nexon remains contextual until a directly cited, exact series can be resolved.

## Pearl Abyss

- Public archive: https://www.pearlabyss.com/en-US/IR/Data/Performance
- Access: no login; official board pages expose presentation or earnings-letter downloads.
- Observed license signal: copyrighted official site and documents; no general open license identified.
- Stability note: attachment URLs may be opaque, so the canonical board-detail page is the preferred citation anchor.
- Redistribution recommendation: store structured facts and exact attachment page numbers. Do not redistribute the full deck by default.

## Sensor Tower and AppMagic

- Phase A.1 did not scrape, copy charts, or ingest numerical values from either service.
- They remain `SECONDARY_CONTEXT` / `INDUSTRY_CONTEXT` only.
- They are not eligible for the public quantitative backbone unless rights are resolved separately.
