## Dark area reduction

Quarter Prism no longer uses a full dark module. The chapter, visualization stage, reading output, and ruler remain on warm mineral/light aqua surfaces. Deep petrol is limited to typography, ridges, local contours, and small measurement marks.

## Line → tide

The shared `TidalBand` grammar renders a filled ribbon plus a precise luminous ridge. Smooth geometry passes through every real quarter anchor; nodes remain visible and interactive reading is restricted to those anchors.

## Selection → aperture

The selected quarter is a locally translucent prism aperture with calibration rails, a reading field, quarter label, ruler alignment, and a contained glow. It does not behave like a free rectangular selection region.

## Quarter selector redesign

Detached quarter boxes are absent. A bottom ruler uses text, ticks, distance-weighted opacity, and previous/next keyboard behavior. The current quarter moves through the instrument rhythm instead of appearing as an admin filter.
