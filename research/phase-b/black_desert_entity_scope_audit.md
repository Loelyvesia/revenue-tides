# Black Desert entity-scope audit

Pearl Abyss labels the series as Black Desert IP revenue. The related reporting materials show platform mixes that include PC, console, and mobile, while the IP revenue row remains aggregated.

The canonical entity is therefore `black_desert_ip` with entity type `IP`. The observation label must not be changed to Black Desert Mobile revenue, Black Desert PC revenue, or one regional edition. Events from an official regional service page may be linked to the IP as context, but they do not allocate the reported amount to that region or platform.
