# Metric versioning audit

Five metric definitions cover the controlled pilot:

1. SHIFT UP operating revenue by IP v1.
2. Lineage M mobile-game revenue legacy v1.
3. Lineage M IP revenue with royalties integrated v2.
4. Pearl Abyss Black Desert IP revenue v1.
5. Netmarble MCOC derived title allocation v1.

Unknown royalty, licensing, or platform-receipt fields remain explicitly `UNKNOWN` unless the source resolves them. A common currency does not create common accounting meaning.

Comparability is conservative. Each stable series is directly comparable to itself across adjacent periods. NIKKE and Stellar Blade share a reporting label but remain `WITHIN_SERIES_ONLY`. The two Lineage M definitions are a `VERSION_BREAK`. Other cross-series pairs are `NOT_COMPARABLE`.
