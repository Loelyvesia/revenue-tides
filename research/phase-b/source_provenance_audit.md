# Source provenance audit

The pilot retains 15 necessary official PDFs: three SHIFT UP documents, four NCSOFT presentations, two Pearl Abyss documents, and six Netmarble presentations. Each retained file has a source ID, canonical official URL, relative internal path, publication/retrieval date, byte size, language, reporting period, and SHA-256.

Six official event pages are kept as URL-only source records. They were not mirrored because deterministic quantitative reconstruction does not depend on their raw HTML.

All quantitative extraction used text/table extraction with exact PDF pages. OCR, screenshots, chart geometry, unofficial mirrors, authenticated endpoints, and private APIs were not used.

The retained PDFs are internal provenance material. The public release layer contains structured factual fields and canonical links rather than copied pages or charts. This is a technical release policy, not a legal opinion.
