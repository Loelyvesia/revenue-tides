# Fiscal period audit

SHIFT UP, NCSOFT, Pearl Abyss, and Netmarble use calendar-year fiscal reporting for the tested 2024Q3–2026Q2 window. This was checked against the dated official quarterly documents and their quarter-ending periods.

The pilot stores one fiscal-period record per company and quarter with exact start/end dates. Every record is labeled `VERIFIED_CALENDAR_ALIGNED`. The model does not assume that future companies or periods share this alignment.

Publication dates remain separate from observation period dates. For example, a February 2025 document can report 2024Q4 without changing the observation period.
