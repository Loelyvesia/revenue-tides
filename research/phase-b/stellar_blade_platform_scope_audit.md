# Stellar Blade platform-scope audit

SHIFT UP reports one continuing Stellar Blade IP-revenue line. The official PlayStation/SHIFT UP announcement dates the PC launch to June 11, 2025, within 2025Q2.

The pilot therefore models two separate facts:

- The revenue metric remains `md_su_ip_revenue_v1` across all eight quarters.
- PC availability begins as a `PLATFORM_LAUNCH` event in 2025Q2.

No source states that the accounting definition changed at launch. The platform expansion is not promoted to a metric-version break. Pre-launch observations are not rewritten as PC-inclusive, and no PS5/PC allocation is inferred.
