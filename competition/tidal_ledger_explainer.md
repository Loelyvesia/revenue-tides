# Tidal Ledger / 潮汐账本

Tidal Ledger treats the site as a measurement ledger rather than a dashboard.

- **Warm mineral paper** provides a quiet reading surface associated with reports and archival records.
- **Graphite ink** carries primary text and rules without the high-gloss tone of a trading interface.
- **Muted teal marks** represent quantitative observations.
- **Oxidized copper rails** isolate events and issuer context from the revenue trajectory.
- **Hydrographic ticks and ledger rules** make period, calibration, and reading order visible.
- **Version seams** show that a reporting definition has changed and prevent false visual continuity.

Literal ocean imagery was avoided because the title's “tide” is a temporal metaphor, not a decorative theme. Game artwork was avoided because it would shift attention from evidence to brands and introduce rights questions. Generic card grids, blue-purple gradients, glassmorphism, and leaderboard panels were avoided because they imply interchangeable metrics and product-performance ranking. The visual system instead makes provenance, discontinuity, and uncertainty part of the page's structure.
