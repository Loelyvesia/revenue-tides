# Limitations

1. Publisher-defined metrics are heterogeneous in entity, platform, accounting, and currency scope.
2. Absolute cross-company revenue comparison and revenue ranking are forbidden.
3. Values are not a uniform measure of global consumer spending or “true global revenue.”
4. NCSOFT legacy and royalty-integrated metric versions cannot be stitched into one trajectory.
5. Netmarble-derived series inherit uncertainty from officially disclosed rounded shares.
6. Event coverage is curated and non-exhaustive.
7. Event timing is contextual and does not establish causality.
8. “Not recovered within observed window” does not mean permanent decline.
9. A real interactive screen-reader session was not available; no full screen-reader or WCAG certification is claimed.
10. WebKit/Safari-equivalent was not available for browser QA.
11. Some remote source URLs may change, redirect, or reject automated access. SHIFT UP's sampled document returned HTTP 403 to automated HEAD while the application link remained syntactically valid; this is a remote-access condition, not a broken application route.

The project also avoids USD normalization, market-share reconstruction, interpolation, forward fill, cross-version QoQ, cross-company leaderboards, and causal language unsupported by the evidence.
