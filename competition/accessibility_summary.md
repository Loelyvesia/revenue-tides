# Accessibility Summary

The production interface passed the accessibility checks available in the final QA environment:

- complete keyboard routes for navigation, Quarter Prism controls, series selection, and source disclosure;
- visible focus, a working skip link, and no observed keyboard trap;
- native buttons and selects with names and state;
- one polite live region for Quarter Prism changes;
- accessible SVG titles/descriptions and adjacent text summaries;
- direct, derived, version-break, event, and selection states encoded with shape, line, text, and position as well as color;
- reduced-motion behavior;
- mobile interactive targets of at least 44 px;
- 200% equivalent reflow without page-level horizontal overflow;
- Chrome accessibility-tree review with one main landmark, one page h1, ordered headings, and named controls.

Audited contrast ratios for primary text, secondary text, event context, and focus treatment met the project's stated targets. A real interactive screen-reader session was **not available**. The project therefore does not claim full screen-reader certification, WCAG certification, or Safari accessibility coverage.
