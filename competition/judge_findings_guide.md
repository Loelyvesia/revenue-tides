# Judge Findings Guide

## F1 — 同一季度，报告口径也会改变数值

- **Meaning:** Reporting definitions alone can change the value shown for the same title and quarter.
- **Period:** 2025Q2–2025Q4 overlap for Lineage M, Lineage 2M, and Lineage W.
- **Key numbers:** Lineage M royalty-integrated values are KRW 6.797–13.455 billion above legacy; the other two overlap differences are zero.
- **Evidence class:** `DESCRIPTIVE`, based on `OFFICIAL_REPORTED` observations.
- **Site location:** Headline Findings and Quarter Prism with Lineage M at 2025Q2.
- **Main limitation:** Difference reflects definitions, not commercial movement.
- **Do not conclude:** Do not call it growth, correction, restatement, or a continuous stitched series.

## F2 — Stellar Blade 的 PC 上线季度形成窗口峰值

- **Meaning:** The eight-quarter window peak coincides with the PC-launch quarter.
- **Period:** 2025Q1–2025Q2.
- **Key numbers:** KRW 7.012 → 65.719 billion; +837.3% QoQ.
- **Evidence class:** `DESCRIPTIVE`, `OFFICIAL_REPORTED` publisher-defined IP revenue.
- **Site location:** Headline Finding F2 and Quarter Prism with Stellar Blade at 2025Q2.
- **Main limitation:** The IP total combines platforms and contains no PC-only amount.
- **Do not conclude:** The PC launch caused the increase or contributed a known amount.

## F3 — NIKKE 反弹，但尚未回到前高

- **Meaning:** A rebound can coexist with non-recovery relative to an earlier peak.
- **Period:** 2025Q2–2026Q2.
- **Key numbers:** +32.4% QoQ in 2026Q2; 4.1% below the 2025Q2 peak.
- **Evidence class:** `DESCRIPTIVE`, `OFFICIAL_REPORTED` IP revenue.
- **Site location:** Remaining Findings; NIKKE detail in Series Observatory.
- **Main limitation:** Event coverage is curated and the window ends at 2026Q2.
- **Do not conclude:** The rebound was caused by the anniversary or the gap is permanent.

## F4 — Black Desert 在一次恢复后再次进入回撤

- **Meaning:** The path contains drawdown, recovery to a new peak, and a second drawdown.
- **Period:** 2024Q4–2026Q2.
- **Key numbers:** 20.7% initial drawdown; recovery/new peak in 2025Q3; 30.8% below that peak by 2026Q2.
- **Evidence class:** `DESCRIPTIVE`, `OFFICIAL_REPORTED` cross-platform IP revenue.
- **Site location:** Headline Finding F4 and Black Desert Series Observatory detail.
- **Main limitation:** Later recovery is outside the observed window.
- **Do not conclude:** Black Desert permanently declined.

## F5 — Lineage W 新口径窗口连续四季下降

- **Meaning:** The separate royalty-integrated v2 window contains four consecutive quarterly declines.
- **Period:** 2025Q2–2026Q2 only.
- **Key numbers:** 47.640 → 30.833 billion KRW; 35.3% below window start.
- **Evidence class:** `DIRECT`, `OFFICIAL_REPORTED`.
- **Site location:** Remaining Findings; Lineage W detail in Series Observatory.
- **Main limitation:** Five-quarter window; cannot extend backward across the metric break.
- **Do not conclude:** Do not stitch legacy to v2 or call the pattern permanent.

## F6 — 取整占比使多数派生环比方向无法确定

- **Meaning:** Rounded official shares can make the direction of a derived change indeterminate.
- **Period:** 2024Q3–2025Q4 for MCOC, Jackpot World, and Lotsa Slots.
- **Key numbers:** 15 eligible changes; 9 sign-sensitive, 6 sign-stable; 60.0% sign-sensitive.
- **Evidence class:** `DERIVED`, `DERIVED_FROM_OFFICIAL`.
- **Site location:** Remaining Findings and Quarter Prism derived-uncertainty state.
- **Main limitation:** Applies to deterministic estimates from rounded official shares, not direct title revenue.
- **Do not conclude:** Issuers are unreliable or sign-sensitive point estimates have definite directions.
